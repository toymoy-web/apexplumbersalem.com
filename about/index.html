Who We Are

A Plumbing Company Focused on This Specific Area

Salem Apex Plumbing serves Salem, Keizer, Woodburn, Dallas, Independence, Silverton, and the surrounding Marion and Polk County area. We work on the plumbing issues that actually come up in this region. That includes the clay soil that stresses sewer lines throughout the Willamette Valley and the mineral content in water drawn from the North Santiam River, rather than applying a generic approach that doesn't account for local conditions.

This regional focus shapes how we actually approach a job, not just where we're willing to drive. A sewer line issue in an older Independence home built near the turn of the century calls for different assumptions than a newer property in a Woodburn subdivision. Treating every job identically regardless of a home's age, materials, and location tends to produce worse outcomes than accounting for those differences from the start.

How We're Licensed

CCB Registered, BCD Licensed Technicians

Oregon splits plumbing licensing between two separate agencies: the Construction Contractors Board (CCB), which registers and bonds the business, and the Building Codes Division (BCD), which licenses individual tradespeople through Journeyman Plumber credentials. Salem Apex Plumbing maintains both, and all work performed follows the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code. We treat verifying both pieces of licensing as a basic standard, not an extra step, and we're glad to answer questions about either directly.

How We Work

Upfront Pricing, No Guesswork

We provide upfront pricing before any work begins, so there are no surprises on the invoice. Estimates are free for non-emergency work, and for emergency dispatches we still provide clear pricing before work starts, not after. We answer calls 24 hours a day, every day, because plumbing emergencies don't wait for business hours, and a burst pipe or a backed-up sewer line at 2 a.m. needs an actual response, not a voicemail.

What We Actually Handle

From Routine Maintenance to Water Damage Response

Our services cover the full range of residential plumbing work. This includes drain cleaning and hydro jetting, water heater repair and installation including tankless systems, sewer line repair and replacement, leak detection, burst and frozen pipe repair, and water damage restoration for the flooding and backup events that occasionally come with Salem's rainy season. A full list of what we handle is on our services page, organized by category rather than as one long undifferentiated list.

We're also honest about what we don't do. If a job falls outside residential and light commercial plumbing, or requires a specialty we don't have in-house, we'll say so directly rather than taking on work we're not equipped to do well.

Where We Work

Salem and the Surrounding Communities

Beyond Salem itself, we regularly serve Keizer, Woodburn, Dallas, Independence, and Silverton, along with the broader Marion and Polk County area. Each of these communities has its own housing stock, soil conditions, and typical plumbing issues, and we treat that as relevant information rather than a detail to ignore. You can find more on our coverage for a specific town on our areas we serve pages.

What Sets Our Approach Apart

Real Diagnosis Before a Recommendation

A recurring theme across the plumbing issues we see, whether it's a sewer line, a water heater, or a drain that keeps clogging, is that the right fix depends on actually understanding the underlying cause, not just addressing the symptom in front of us. A camera inspection before recommending sewer line work, testing actual water hardness before sizing a softener, and confirming pipe material before quoting a repiping job are all part of the same basic principle. We give you a recommendation based on your specific situation, not a generic default.

This sometimes means telling a homeowner that a smaller repair will genuinely hold up, rather than upselling a larger job that isn't actually necessary yet. It also means being direct when a patch-repair approach has reached the point of diminishing returns and a more complete fix is the more honest recommendation, even if that's a harder conversation than simply doing the smaller job again.

Communication and Scheduling

Clear Timelines, Not Vague Windows

We aim to give realistic arrival windows and keep you informed if something changes, rather than a vague "sometime today" that leaves you unable to plan around it. For emergency dispatches, our priority is getting a licensed technician to your property as quickly as reasonably possible given current call volume and location, and we communicate honestly about expected timing rather than making promises we can't reliably keep.

Questions Before You Call

We're Glad to Talk Through It First

If you're not sure whether something is an emergency, whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense, or what a job is likely to involve, calling and asking is a normal part of how we work, not an imposition. We'd rather give you a clear, honest answer over the phone than have you guess and end up with the wrong expectation going into a job.
