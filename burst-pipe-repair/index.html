What to Do Right Now

Shut Off the Main Valve

Stop the water at the source first, this limits damage more than anything else you can do.

Smell Gas?

Leave the house immediately and call from outside. Do not operate any switches.

Turn Off Electricity Near Standing Water

If water is pooling near outlets or a panel, shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Move Valuables Away From the Water

Once the water is shut off, move furniture and belongings out of the affected area if it's safe to do so.

Local Cause

Why Pipes Burst in Salem

Salem's wet season runs roughly October through March, and while the Willamette Valley's winters are milder than much of the country, hard freezes still happen, typically in short cold snaps between December and February. Supply lines running through unheated crawl spaces, garages, and exterior walls are the most vulnerable, since they don't get the ambient warmth of the living space above them. A pipe that freezes and then thaws can crack along its length even if it never fully bursts during the freeze itself.

Older galvanized supply lines are also more prone to splitting under normal water pressure as they corrode internally over decades, independent of any freeze event, which is why a burst pipe call in July is just as real as one in January.

Prevention

Protecting Pipes Before the Next Cold Snap

Insulating exposed pipes in crawl spaces, garages, and exterior walls is the single most effective step before Salem's occasional December through February freeze. Letting a faucet drip slightly on the coldest nights keeps water moving through the line, which makes it much harder for ice to form a full blockage. Knowing where your main shutoff valve is located before an emergency happens saves critical minutes when every second matters.

Homes in Highland, Grant, and other older neighborhoods with original supply lines running through unheated crawl spaces see this problem more than newer construction with better-insulated plumbing runs.
