Hours

Open 24 Hours, Every Day

We answer calls around the clock, including nights, weekends, and holidays, because plumbing emergencies don't follow a business-hours schedule. Whether it's a burst pipe at midnight or a water heater that stopped working on a Sunday morning, calling gets you an actual response, not a message to leave and wait on.

What to Expect When You Call

A Straightforward Process

When you call, we'll ask a few questions about what's happening so we can dispatch the right technician with the right equipment for the job. For emergency situations, we prioritize getting someone to your property quickly. For non-emergency work, we'll schedule a visit at a time that works for you and provide an upfront estimate before any work begins. There's never a form to fill out or a chatbot to talk to first, just a direct conversation with someone who can actually help.

Service Area

Where We Work

Beyond Salem, we regularly serve Keizer, Woodburn, Dallas, Independence, and Silverton, along with the surrounding Marion and Polk County area. If you're outside these areas and unsure whether we cover your location, give us a call and we'll let you know directly.
