Local Cause

Why Garbage Disposals Jam More in Cool, Wet Climates

Salem's cool, damp climate means cooking grease and oils solidify faster inside kitchen drain connections than they would in a warmer climate. That solidified grease combines with food particles to jam the disposal's grinding chamber or clog the connecting drain line just past the unit, which is why disposal calls often overlap with kitchen drain cleaning calls in Salem specifically.

Symptoms

Signs You Need Disposal Repair

Humming But Not Grinding

Usually a jammed impeller, often fixable without replacing the unit.

Leaking From the Bottom

A worn seal or cracked housing that typically means replacement, not repair.

Frequent Jamming

Can point to grinding components wearing out or grease buildup in the connecting line.

Unusual Noise or Smell

Grinding noise or persistent odor often means trapped debris or a failing motor.

Resetting Repeatedly

A reset button that needs pressing often means the motor is overheating under strain.

Repair or Replace

When a New Disposal Makes More Sense

A jammed impeller or a tripped reset button is usually a quick, low-cost repair. A leaking housing, a failing motor, or a unit that's more than 8 to 10 years old is a different situation where replacement is typically the better value, especially since disposal technology and grinding efficiency have improved over that timeframe.

Installation

New Disposal Installation

We install disposals sized to your household's typical use, properly connected to the drain line and dishwasher hookup where applicable. All new installations meet current electrical code, and we verify the connecting drain line is clear before considering the job complete, since a partially clogged line downstream can make a brand-new disposal seem like it's malfunctioning.

Prevention

Keeping a Disposal Running Longer

Running cold water while the disposal operates helps solidify grease into small pieces that pass through rather than coating the grinding chamber. Avoiding fibrous scraps like celery and corn husks, along with grease and oil, prevents the two most common causes of jamming. For a unit that jams repeatedly despite reasonable use, that's usually a sign of wear rather than a habits problem.

All installation and repair work follows the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and current electrical code for the disposal's power connection.

FAQs

Common Questions

My disposal hums but doesn't grind. What's wrong?

That's usually a jammed impeller, which is often fixable by clearing the jam rather than replacing the whole unit.

How long do garbage disposals typically last?

Most units last 8 to 10 years with normal use. Frequent jamming or a leaking housing past that point usually means replacement is the better value.

Why does my new disposal still seem clogged?

A partially blocked drain line downstream of the disposal can make a working unit seem like it's malfunctioning. We check the connecting line as part of every installation.

Can I put grease down the disposal?

We recommend avoiding it. Grease solidifies inside the drain line, especially in Salem's cooler climate, and is one of the most common causes of disposal and drain clogs.
