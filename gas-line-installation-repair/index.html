When You Need This

Common Reasons for Gas Line Work

Gas line work comes up most often around a tankless water heater upgrade, since tankless units typically need a larger gas supply than a standard tank. It also comes up for new gas ranges, fireplace installations, and outdoor grill or fire pit hookups, along with repairs when a line is leaking, damaged, or simply too old to trust.

Safety First

What to Do If You Smell Gas

Leave Immediately

Get everyone out of the house and away from the building.

Don't Use Switches or Phones Inside

Avoid anything that could create a spark near a gas leak.

Call From Outside

Call us or your gas utility once you're safely away from the structure.

Don't Try to Locate It Yourself

Leave leak detection to a technician with the right equipment.

Don't Turn Off Gas Yourself Unless Trained

If you know how to safely shut off the meter valve, do so, otherwise leave it to responders.

Installation

Sizing a Gas Line Correctly

Gas line sizing depends on the total demand of everything connected to it, which is why upgrading to a tankless water heater often means resizing the line rather than just adding a new branch off the existing one. Undersized gas lines cause appliances to underperform or shut off under load, which is a common and frustrating surprise after a tankless installation that didn't account for it upfront.

Before quoting any new gas appliance installation, we calculate the total demand of everything already on the line, including the water heater, furnace, range, and dryer where applicable, to confirm the existing line can actually support one more appliance without upsizing.

Code Compliance

Gas Work Done to Code

All gas line installation and repair follows the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code and applicable fuel gas code requirements, with permitting and final inspection handled as part of the project. Gas work isn't an area to cut corners on, since an improperly sized or poorly sealed line creates a real safety risk rather than just a performance issue, which is why we pressure test every repair and new installation before considering the job complete.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need a permit for gas line work?

Yes, gas line installation and most repairs require a permit and inspection, which we handle as part of the project.

Why does my tankless water heater underperform after installation?

An undersized gas line is a common cause. Tankless units typically need more gas supply than a standard tank, and the existing line isn't always resized to match.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the house immediately, avoid using switches or phones inside, and call from outside once you're safely away from the building.

Can you run a gas line for an outdoor fire pit or grill?

Yes, we size and run gas lines for outdoor fixtures the same way we do for appliances inside the home.
