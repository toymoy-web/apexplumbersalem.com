Not Sure Where It's Coming From?

If Your Water Bill Jumped and You Don't Know Why

If your water meter keeps running even with every fixture in the house turned off, or your bill spiked and nothing obvious changed, that's the classic sign of a hidden leak rather than a visible one. Most people call us not because they've found a leak, but because they know something's wrong and can't find where. That's exactly what leak detection is built to answer.

Local Cause

Why Hidden Leaks Are Common in Salem

Salem's clay soil movement stresses buried water service lines the same way it stresses sewer laterals, and a pinhole leak in a line running under a slab or through a wall can run for months before it becomes visible. Older copper supply lines, common in homes built before the 1980s, are also prone to pinhole leaks as they age, even without any soil movement involved.

How It Works

Electronic Leak Tracing

We use acoustic and electronic leak-tracing equipment to locate the leak without guessing or digging blindly. This method listens for the specific sound water makes escaping under pressure and can pinpoint a leak's location through concrete, drywall, and soil, which means we can usually confirm the exact spot before any repair work begins.

If the equipment points to a leak specifically under a concrete slab, that's a distinct diagnostic and repair situation from a leak inside a wall or under a yard, and our slab leak detection and repair team handles that specialized case. All repair work follows the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

While You Wait

What to Do Before We Arrive

If you can see or hear active water flow somewhere you can safely access, shutting off the main valve stops the leak entirely while we schedule the visit. If the leak is only showing up as a meter reading or a bill increase with no visible water anywhere, there's no urgent action needed, just note when you first noticed the change so we can narrow down the timeline.

Response Time

Emergency or Scheduled Service

A meter that's been quietly running for weeks with no visible water damage can usually wait for a scheduled leak-tracing appointment. Active water intrusion, a ceiling that's sagging or dripping, or any sign the leak is actively damaging the structure right now is different, and we treat that as an emergency dispatch. FAQs

Common Questions

My water bill went up and I don't know why. Could it be a leak?

Yes, that's one of the most common reasons we get called. An unexplained bill increase with no change in household water use is a strong sign of a hidden leak.

Will you have to tear open my walls or floors to find the leak?

Usually not. Acoustic and electronic leak-tracing equipment pinpoints the leak's location first, so any opening we make is targeted rather than exploratory.

How is a slab leak different from a regular hidden leak?

A slab leak specifically involves a pipe running under a concrete foundation, which needs different equipment and repair access than a leak inside a wall or yard.

Is a hidden leak covered by insurance?

A sudden, accidental leak is often covered. A slow leak that went undetected for a long time typically isn't. Check your specific policy.
