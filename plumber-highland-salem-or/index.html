The Neighborhood

Plumbing Service in Highland, North of Downtown Salem

Highland is an older, urbanized part of Salem just north of downtown. The Willamette River lies along its west side and Keizer sits to the north. In 2021 the Salem Historic Landmarks Commission added Highland to its list of Heritage Neighborhoods, alongside Grant and Southeast Salem.

Real estate listing data, which we treat as supporting evidence rather than an official count, describes Highland as mostly older cottages and bungalows. It puts the median year built around 1940 and the typical single-family home at roughly 1,275 square feet. Those homes sit close together on sidewalk streets, and attached garages are described as rare.

What That Means for a Service Call

Smaller Homes Call for Careful Planning

A smaller home usually means compact spaces for the water heater, the main shutoff, and the drain lines. That does not make a repair harder in itself, but it does mean measuring access before a replacement is scheduled. If you are considering a new water heater, tell us where the current one sits and what room is around it.

Houses of this age have often been updated in stages, so the plumbing you see in the kitchen may not match what serves the bathroom. We look at each fixture and drain on its own terms instead of assuming one pattern for the whole house. When a repair is enough, we will say so.

FAQ

Highland Plumbing Questions

Where is Highland in Salem?

Highland is just north of downtown Salem. The Willamette River forms its western edge and Keizer lies to the north.

What kind of homes are in Highland?

Real estate listing data describes mostly older cottages and bungalows, with a median build year around 1940. Treat that as a general picture, since every house has its own history of repairs and updates.

Is Highland a Heritage Neighborhood?

Yes. The Salem Historic Landmarks Commission designated Highland a Heritage Neighborhood in 2021. The City describes the program as a way to help neighborhoods celebrate their history.

Can you replace a water heater in a small utility space?

Often yes, but we need to know the size of the space and how the current unit is vented or connected. Share that when you call so the replacement can be planned before the visit.

Do you serve Keizer as well as Highland?

Yes. Keizer has a dedicated page, and we serve both areas.
