The Neighborhood

Homes and Businesses Side by Side

The Southeast Salem Neighborhood Association, known as SESNA, covers the area bounded by State Street on the north, Interstate 5 on the east, 25th Street on the south, and Mission Street on the west and northwest. The Salem Historic Landmarks Commission named it the city's second Heritage Neighborhood in 2015.

The City of Salem says the neighborhood has more than 2,000 houses, many of them built in the late 1800s. It is not only residential. The same area holds state government offices, Salem-Keizer School District offices, the Willamette Heritage Center, the Salem Train Station and Freight Depot, and about 500 businesses.

Residential and Commercial

Why the Mix Matters for Plumbing

A neighborhood with both older houses and working businesses brings different plumbing questions into the same few blocks. A homeowner may be dealing with a slow drain or an aging water heater. A shop or office may need backflow testing, a restroom repair, or a drain that keeps up with daily use.

We handle both kinds of calls, and we will tell you plainly which service fits. Where a building is historic, it is worth asking the City of Salem about any review that applies to exterior changes before work begins.

FAQ

Southeast Salem Plumbing Questions

What are the boundaries of Southeast Salem?

The City of Salem lists State Street on the north, Interstate 5 on the east, 25th Street on the south, and Mission Street on the west and northwest. A neighborhood map on the City's site shows the detail.

Is Southeast Salem a historic neighborhood?

Yes. The Salem Historic Landmarks Commission named it a Heritage Neighborhood in 2015, and the City says it has more than 2,000 houses, many built in the late 1800s.

Do you work on businesses as well as homes there?

Yes. Southeast Salem includes about 500 businesses, along with state and school district offices, and we handle both residential and commercial plumbing.

Does my property need backflow testing?

It depends on the property and the type of water service or equipment it has. Call us and describe the setup, and we can tell you whether a test is likely to apply.

Do you serve the neighborhoods around Southeast Salem?

Yes. We serve the wider Salem area, including Grant to the north and South Central Salem to the west.
