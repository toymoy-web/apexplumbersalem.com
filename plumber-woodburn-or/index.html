Housing Era

Woodburn's 1960s and 70s Housing Stock

Woodburn was platted in 1871 and incorporated in 1889, but the majority of the city's current housing dates from the 1960s and 1970s, built during a wave of growth well after the original railroad-era downtown was established. That generation of homes is now old enough that original galvanized supply lines, aging water heaters, and early-generation fixtures are common sources of service calls, especially in neighborhoods built before PEX and modern PVC became standard.

Founder Jesse H. Settlemier platted the town after the railroad arrived, and Woodburn grew as a transportation and agricultural hub through the late 19th and early 20th centuries. That layered history means service calls here span everything from genuinely old downtown infrastructure to the more common postwar-era plumbing found throughout most of the city's residential neighborhoods. Interstate 5 and Oregon Routes 211, 214, 219, and 99E all pass through or near Woodburn, which keeps us within easy reach regardless of which part of the city a call comes from.

The older downtown core, closer to the original 1871 townsite platted by founder Jesse H. Settlemier, carries an even earlier layer of infrastructure, with some buildings and homes dating well before the postwar growth wave.

Diagnosis

What We Check First in a Woodburn Home

For an older Woodburn property, we start with a visual and camera check of accessible supply lines before recommending anything, since galvanized pipe corrosion is often visible at joints and fittings well before it causes a visible leak. Catching that early gives homeowners the choice between planned repiping and waiting for a series of unpredictable failures. The same logic applies to water heaters from that era, since a unit installed during the original 1960s or 70s construction wave is almost certainly well past any reasonable service life today.

Questions From Woodburn Homeowners

Why does my Woodburn home need so many plumbing repairs for its age?

Homes from Woodburn's 1960s and 70s growth era often still have original galvanized supply lines, which corrode internally over decades regardless of how well the home has otherwise been maintained.

Do you serve businesses near the outlet mall?

Yes, we handle commercial plumbing throughout Woodburn, including retail and hospitality properties near the outlet center and agricultural processing facilities elsewhere in the city.

Do you charge extra to come to Woodburn?

Is emergency plumbing available in Woodburn at night?

Yes, our emergency line covers Woodburn 24/7, the same as Salem and Keizer.

Is galvanized pipe actually a real problem, or just a talking point?

It's real. Galvanized steel corrodes from the inside out over decades, narrowing the pipe's interior and eventually causing pinhole leaks, often in more than one spot once it starts.
