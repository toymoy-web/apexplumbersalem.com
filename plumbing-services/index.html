Salem's volcanic clay soil, hard water from the North Santiam River supply, and aging pipe stock in the city's older neighborhoods drive most of what we're called out for. Below is the full list of services we provide, grouped by what they address, so you can find the right fix for what's actually happening at your property. If nothing below matches exactly, call (503) 974-3329 and describe it, most plumbing problems fall under one of these categories even if the symptom doesn't obviously point to it.

That includes Salem's older core neighborhoods, Highland, Grant, Morningside, and South Salem, where original mid-century plumbing is common, as well as the newer growth areas in Four Corners and Hayesville on the east side and West Salem across the river in Polk County.

FAQs

Common Questions

I'm not sure which service matches my problem. What should I do?

Call (503) 974-3329 and describe what's happening. We'll point you to the right service and give you an honest read on whether it's an emergency before anyone comes out.

Do you offer emergency service for all of these?

Our emergency line covers burst pipes, no hot water, sewer backups, and active water damage 24/7. Non-urgent work like fixture installs and water quality upgrades is typically scheduled during the day.

Do you charge for estimates?

Estimates for scheduled, non-emergency work are free. For active emergencies, you get an upfront price before any work begins rather than a separate estimate visit.

What areas do you serve besides Salem?

We dispatch throughout Marion and Polk County, including Keizer, Woodburn, Dallas, Independence, and Silverton.
