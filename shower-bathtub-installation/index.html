Common Reasons

Why Homeowners Upgrade Showers and Tubs

A bathroom remodel is the most common reason we install new showers and tubs, but it's not the only one. Aging fixtures in Salem's older housing stock often show mineral staining and grout deterioration from years of hard water exposure, which no amount of cleaning fully reverses. Aging-in-place conversions, replacing a tub with a curbless walk-in shower, are also increasingly common as homeowners plan for long-term accessibility.

A cracked or chronically leaking tub or shower pan is another common trigger, since a failing pan can let water seep into subfloor and framing long before the leak becomes visible from inside the bathroom.

Water Quality

Protecting a New Installation From Hard Water

A brand-new shower or tub is subject to the same mineral buildup that wore out the old one if nothing else changes. Pairing a new installation with a whole-house water softener, or at minimum a shower filter, helps preserve grout, glass, and fixture finishes longer than they'd otherwise last given Salem's water mineral content. It's worth discussing at the time of installation rather than as an afterthought once staining starts to reappear.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you convert my tub to a walk-in shower?

Yes, tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common installation projects, especially for accessibility-focused remodels.

Do you work with my contractor or tile installer?

Yes, we regularly coordinate plumbing work around tile setting and general remodel timelines on larger projects.

What if you find water damage behind my old shower?

We'll document it and discuss the real scope before proceeding, since hidden damage sometimes changes the project timeline and cost. In some cases this connects to our water damage restoration team if drying or structural repair is needed first.

How long does a shower or tub installation take?

A straightforward fixture swap can take a day. A full conversion or remodel-integrated project takes longer depending on the scope.

Should I add a water softener when I install a new shower?

It's worth considering. A softener helps preserve grout, glass, and fixture finishes longer than they'd otherwise last given Salem's water mineral content.
