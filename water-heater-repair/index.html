Local Cause

Why Salem Water Heaters Wear Out the Way They Do

Salem's municipal water comes from the North Santiam River, treated through one of the largest slow-sand filtration systems in the country. The water itself is generally clean, but dissolved minerals still travel through the system and settle inside a water heater tank over years of use. That sediment layer insulates the heating element from the water above it, forcing the unit to run longer to reach the same temperature and shortening the life of tanks that have never been flushed.

Homes in older parts of Salem, particularly those built before the 1980s, are also more likely to be running a tank that's well past its 10 to 12 year expected lifespan, simply because nobody's replaced it since the original install.

By Unit Type

Tank, Tankless, and Heat Pump Units

Standard tank water heaters are the most common repair call, and most issues trace back to the thermostat, heating element, gas control valve, or pressure relief valve. Tankless units fail differently, usually from mineral scale building up on the heat exchanger or a fouled flow sensor rather than a full tank of sediment, and they need their own annual descaling service to keep running efficiently. Heat pump water heaters add a compressor and fan to the mix, so a no-heat call on one of those units gets a different diagnostic path than a standard tank. We service Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Rinnai, and Navien units, tank and tankless alike.

Diagnosis

How We Diagnose the Problem

We start by checking the thermostat, heating element or gas valve, and pressure relief valve, since those three components cause the majority of repair calls. For tank units, we also check for sediment buildup and signs of tank corrosion. For tankless units, we check the flow sensor and heat exchanger for scale buildup, which is the tankless equivalent of the same mineral problem.

Repair or Replace

Repair vs. Replacement

Not every water heater problem needs a new unit. A failed thermostat, heating element, or pressure relief valve on a tank under about 8 years old is usually a straightforward, lower-cost repair. A leaking tank, heavy internal corrosion, or a unit already past 10 to 12 years old is a different situation, since the tank itself is failing rather than one replaceable part.

Repair Makes Sense When

  • Unit is under 8 years old
  • Problem is one specific part
  • Tank itself isn't leaking or corroded

Replacement Makes Sense When

  • Tank is leaking or visibly corroded
  • Unit is over 10 to 12 years old
  • Repairs are becoming frequent

If replacement turns out to be the honest answer, our water heater installation & replacement team handles the swap, including haul-away of the old unit.

Maintenance

Extending the Life of Your Unit

An annual tank flush clears out settled sediment before it has a chance to insulate the heating element, and it's the single most effective thing a homeowner can do given Salem's mineral content. Checking the anode rod every two to three years catches corrosion protection that's wearing out before it turns into a tank leak. Tankless units benefit from an annual descaling flush for the same reason, just applied to the heat exchanger instead of a tank.

Expectations

How Long Water Heaters Actually Last

A standard tank water heater typically lasts 10 to 12 years with reasonable maintenance, less if it's never been flushed in a mineral-heavy water supply like Salem's. Tankless units generally last longer, often 15 to 20 years, since there's no tank to corrode, though the heat exchanger still needs periodic descaling. A heat pump water heater sits in between, with a compressor that adds a wear component a standard tank doesn't have. None of these figures are a guarantee for any specific unit, they're just what typically shows up across normal households.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you repair water heaters at night or on weekends?

Yes. No hot water is one of our most common emergency calls, so our line is answered 24/7.

Why does my water heater need repairs so often in Salem?

Dissolved minerals in the North Santiam River water supply accumulate inside the tank over years, insulating the heating element and shortening the unit's life.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

A unit under about 8 years old with one failed component is usually a repair. A leaking, corroded, or 10-plus-year-old tank is usually better replaced.

Is a leaking water heater covered by homeowners insurance?

Sudden, accidental leaks are often covered. Gradual leaks from age typically aren't. Check your specific policy.

How long does a typical repair take?

Most repairs are completed in a single visit since we carry the common replacement parts. A special-order part for a newer tankless or heat pump unit may need a short follow-up.

Do you work on tankless and heat pump water heaters, or just tanks?

All three. Tankless and heat pump units fail differently than a standard tank, so we diagnose them with a different process, but we service all major brands and types.
